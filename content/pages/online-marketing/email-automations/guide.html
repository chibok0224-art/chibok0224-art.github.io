What a good email automation package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Flows and number of emails.
  • Triggers and conditions.
  • Copy and design, if included.
  • Integration with store or CRM.
  • Testing of all branches.
  • Tracking and reporting.
  • Compliance, timeline and support.

Ask for a flow diagram showing triggers, wait times, conditions and exits. It helps you understand what customers receive and spot gaps, such as a customer getting a discount after already buying.

Request a test log: which scenarios were tested and the results. Testing all paths prevents embarrassing mistakes like sending cart reminders after a purchase.

Look at flows the expert has built for businesses like yours and ask what results they produced. Clear numbers such as recovered carts or repeat purchases, explained honestly, show they focus on outcomes rather than on the number of emails sent.

How to brief an email automation expert

  1. Platform and connected tools.
  2. Flows you want.
  3. Customer journey and key actions.
  4. Brand voice and design.
  5. Offers and rules, such as discount limits.
  6. Current list size and consent.
  7. Goals and deadline.

Start with a few high-value flows and do them well. Three tested automations bring more than ten rushed ones with errors.

Write rules for offers: who gets a discount, when and how often. Without rules, automations can train customers to wait for discounts.

Keep emails useful, not only promotional. Tips, order updates and helpful content build trust and keep unsubscribes low.

Review results after a month and adjust timing, subject lines or content. Small changes often improve results noticeably.

Plan how automations interact with regular campaigns. Customers in a welcome flow may not need a weekly newsletter on the same day. Simple rules that pause or space out messages prevent overwhelming new subscribers.

What drives the price

  • number of flows and emails
  • copywriting and design
  • integration complexity
  • segmentation and personalization
  • testing and analytics
  • ongoing optimization

Setting up a welcome series costs much less than a full automation program with many flows, segments and monthly optimization.

Platform subscription costs are separate and grow with list size.

Red flags

  • No testing of triggers.
  • No flow diagrams.
  • Ignores consent and unsubscribes.
  • Discounts in every email.
  • No tracking of results.

Check flows after any change to your store, products or platform. Updates can break triggers quietly.

Tips for a smoother project

Keep a record of each flow's purpose and settings for future team members.

Check how automations look on phones, since most people read email on mobile. Short subject lines, readable text and large buttons matter, and a quick test on your own phone often reveals layout problems.
