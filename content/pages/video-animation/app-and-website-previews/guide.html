What a good preview video package includes

Preview video gigs are priced by length, number of versions and effects. Check that the package states:

  • Platforms: App Store, Google Play, website or ads.
  • Sizes and lengths required by each.
  • Footage: screen recordings from your app.
  • Text and captions.
  • Music, licensed.
  • Store compliance.
  • Revisions.

Ask for a storyboard or shot list before editing. Agreeing on the order of features and messages keeps the preview focused on what matters most.

Ask for a clear poster frame or first frame. On many store pages the video does not play until tapped, and the still image is what most visitors see. A frame that shows the app's main screen and benefit clearly works like an extra screenshot and can influence whether people tap to watch at all.

How to brief a preview video creator

  1. The main benefit of your app or site.
  2. Top three features to show.
  3. Screen recordings or access to a test build.
  4. Brand elements: colors, fonts, logo.
  5. Platforms and required sizes.
  6. Localization needs.

Show real actions, not just menus. A preview that shows someone completing a task, like booking or creating something, explains the app far better than scrolling through screens.

Use realistic sample data. Empty screens or placeholder text make the app look unfinished. Clean, believable data makes it look ready to use.

Consider localized versions for key markets. On-screen text in the viewer's language can improve results in those stores.

What drives the price

  • number of platforms and sizes
  • length and complexity
  • motion graphics and device mockups
  • localized versions
  • music and voice-over
  • turnaround time

Store-compliant versions for several device sizes add work. Ask for all needed sizes in one order rather than adding them later.

Red flags

  • Showing features that do not exist.
  • Ignoring store length and size rules.
  • Tiny text that cannot be read on a phone.
  • Music with no license.
  • Screen recordings with personal data visible.

Check the preview on the store listing after upload. Thumbnails, poster frames and autoplay behavior affect what users actually see first.

Plan updates with your release cycle. When the app's interface or key features change, an outdated preview makes the listing feel stale. Keeping the project files and a simple template makes it easy to update the preview whenever you ship a major redesign.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I know the main benefit and top features.
  • I have the store size and length requirements.
  • Footage shows real actions with realistic data.
  • Text is readable on phones and works without sound.
  • Music is licensed.
