What a good real estate video package includes

Real estate video gigs are priced by length, source material and formats. Check that the package states:

  • Source: your footage, photos or drone clips.
  • Length and versions for listings and social.
  • Editing: color, stabilization, transitions.
  • Text: price, address, features, contact.
  • Music, licensed for commercial use.
  • Branding: agent or agency elements.
  • Turnaround.

Ask for a consistent template for all your listings. A recognizable style builds your brand, and it makes each new video faster and cheaper to produce.

Ask for a short version for social media that highlights the best features in the first seconds, such as a striking view, a bright kitchen or a garden. Social viewers scroll quickly, and the strongest feature should appear before they move on. The full walkthrough can live on the listing page.

How to brief a real estate video editor

  1. Footage and photos, organized by room.
  2. Property details: key features, size and location highlights.
  3. Order of rooms for a natural walkthrough.
  4. Branding: logo, colors, contact details.
  5. Platforms and formats.
  6. Deadline, often tied to the listing date.

Film in good light and steady movements. Editors can improve footage, but shaky, dark clips limit what is possible. A simple stabilizer and daylight make a big difference.

Keep the video honest. Show the property as it is, including the rooms buyers will care about most. Misleading edits lead to disappointed viewings and can damage your reputation.

Add a short neighborhood section if possible: nearby parks, shops or transport. Buyers care about location, and short clips can answer those questions.

What drives the price

  • length and number of versions
  • amount of footage to edit
  • photo-only slideshows versus video
  • text, graphics and branding
  • drone and aerial footage
  • turnaround time

Fast turnaround matters in real estate. Ask whether the editor can deliver quickly for new listings, and what that costs.

Red flags

  • Editing that hides defects or changes features.
  • Music with no license.
  • No vertical version for social media.
  • Missing or wrong property details.
  • Slow turnaround for time-sensitive listings.

Check every figure and address in the video before publishing. Wrong prices or details in property marketing can cause disputes and may break local advertising rules.

Tips for a smoother project

Include captions or on-screen labels for key rooms and features, since many people watch without sound. Clear labels such as room names and sizes help viewers understand the layout, and they make the video useful for buyers who cannot visit the property in person.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My footage and photos are organized by room.
  • The video will show the property honestly.
  • Music is licensed.
  • I will get listing and vertical versions.
  • I will check every detail before publishing.
