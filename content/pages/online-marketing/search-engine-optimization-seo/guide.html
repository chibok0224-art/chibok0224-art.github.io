Know which kind of SEO you need

"SEO" covers very different jobs. Pick the one that matches your problem:

  • SEO audit: a review of your site with a prioritized list of problems. The best first order for most sites.
  • Technical SEO: speed, indexing, broken pages, redirects, structured data, sitemaps.
  • On-page SEO: titles, headings, internal links and content matched to the right keywords.
  • Keyword research: which searches your customers actually use, and which ones you can realistically rank for.
  • Local SEO: your Google Business Profile, reviews and local listings, for businesses that serve an area.
  • Link building: getting other sites to link to yours. Valuable when done well, risky when done in bulk.

What good deliverables look like

Ask for concrete outputs you can check, for example:

  • an audit report with each issue, why it matters and how to fix it, ranked by priority
  • a keyword spreadsheet with search intent and a suggested page for each keyword
  • a list of the exact changes made to your site, if the seller implements fixes
  • for link building, a list of the sites where links were placed

What to share before ordering

  1. Your website address and platform (WordPress, Shopify, Wix and so on).
  2. Your goal: more leads, more sales, more local calls, more readers.
  3. Target country and language.
  4. Two or three competitors who rank where you want to rank.
  5. Current data, if you have it: Google Search Console or analytics access with limited permissions.

What drives the price

  • size of the site and number of pages covered
  • scope: report only, or report plus implementation
  • one-off project versus monthly ongoing work
  • competitiveness of your market and keywords

Red flags

  • Guaranteed rankings, such as "#1 on Google in 30 days".
  • Thousands of backlinks for a small fixed price.
  • Traffic packages that send visitors from bots or unrelated countries.
  • Requests for your main account password instead of a user role.
  • No sample report or examples of past work.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I know which kind of SEO work I am buying.
  • The gig lists concrete deliverables I can check.
  • I have seen a sample report or case study.
  • No one is promising a guaranteed ranking.
  • I will grant access through user roles, not my password.
