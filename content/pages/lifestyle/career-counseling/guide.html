What a good career coaching package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Session format and number.
  • Focus areas.
  • Assessments, if used.
  • Action plan.
  • Interview practice.
  • Confidentiality.
  • Pricing.

Ask for a written action plan after the first session with concrete steps and dates. Plans turn conversations into progress.

Request mock interviews with feedback if you are job hunting. Practice builds confidence quickly.

Ask coaches about people they have helped in situations like yours, such as changing industries or returning to work. Relevant experience means more practical advice and realistic timelines.

How to brief a career coach

  1. Your current situation.
  2. Goals.
  3. Experience and CV.
  4. Industries of interest.
  5. Constraints: location, time, income.
  6. Questions.
  7. Timeline.

Be honest about constraints, such as needing a stable income or limited time for study. Realistic plans fit your life.

Bring specific job ads or roles you are considering. Concrete examples help the coach give targeted advice.

Choose a coach with knowledge of your field or the field you want to enter.

Prepare between sessions by completing tasks, such as researching roles or contacting people in your network. Progress depends on action, and coaching works best when sessions review real steps you have taken.

Ask how the coach helps with confidence and motivation as well as strategy. Career changes and job searches can be discouraging, and a coach who helps you handle rejection and keep momentum adds as much value as advice on CVs and interviews.

What drives the price

  • number of sessions
  • coach experience
  • assessments
  • interview practice
  • CV and profile help
  • industry specialization

A single session costs much less than a multi-week program with assessments, practice interviews and follow-up support.

Red flags

  • Job guarantees.
  • Generic advice.
  • No action plan.
  • Pressure to buy long packages.
  • No confidentiality.

Review your plan monthly and adjust as you learn from applications and conversations.

Tips for a smoother project

Think about learning needs, such as short courses or certifications, and ask the coach to prioritize the most valuable ones for your goal.

Consider informational conversations with people in your target field. Coaches can help you prepare questions and contact people respectfully, and these conversations often reveal opportunities that never appear in job ads.

Keep a simple log of applications, conversations and results to discuss with your coach.

Think about timing. Career changes often take months, especially if new skills or certifications are needed. A coach can help you plan a realistic timeline with milestones, so you can keep earning while you prepare and move when the right opportunity appears.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My goals and constraints are clear.
  • I will get an action plan.
  • Interview practice is included if needed.
  • Nobody guarantees a job.
  • Confidentiality is agreed.
