What a good resume design package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Number of pages and layout options.
  • ATS-friendly structure with real, selectable text.
  • Editable source file in a format you can use.
  • PDF export ready to send.
  • Extras such as cover letter or LinkedIn banner.
  • Fonts that are free or included.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

Ask for the editable file in software you already have. A resume you cannot update yourself becomes a problem the next time you apply for a job.

Request a plain-text check: copy all text from the PDF and paste it into a simple editor. If the order is jumbled or parts are missing, an ATS may have the same trouble reading it.

Look at the designer's samples as a recruiter would. Can you find the job title, recent role and key skills within a few seconds? If the eye wanders around decorations first, the layout may look attractive but slow down the people who decide whether to call you.

How to brief a resume designer

  1. Your current resume or all the content in a document.
  2. Target roles and industry.
  3. Country where you apply, since norms differ.
  4. Style: conservative, modern or creative.
  5. Examples of layouts you like.
  6. File format you need to edit.
  7. Deadline.

Finalize your content before design starts. Changing text after the layout is built can break spacing and cost extra revisions.

Tell the designer which achievements matter most. Good design guides the eye to your strongest points, such as results, skills or certifications, instead of giving every line equal weight.

If you work in a creative field, you can show more personality. For most other roles, a clean, readable layout with clear headings is the safer choice.

What drives the price

  • number of pages
  • custom design versus template
  • editable formats requested
  • matching cover letter and extras
  • content rewriting included
  • urgent delivery

Customizing a proven layout costs less than a fully original design with icons, charts and multiple versions.

Red flags

  • Text placed inside images or graphics.
  • No editable file offered.
  • Skill bars or charts that hide real information.
  • Fonts you cannot use on your own computer.
  • Promises of guaranteed interviews.

Keep your design simple enough that you can update it in a few minutes. You will likely tailor it for each job you apply for.

Tips for a smoother project

Save each tailored version with a clear file name, such as your name, the role and the date. Recruiters receive many files named resume, and a tidy name makes yours easier to find and shows care before they even open it.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My content is final before design starts.
  • The layout is ATS-friendly with real text.
  • I will receive an editable file I can open.
  • The style suits my country and industry.
  • Fonts are free or included.
