What a good affiliate program package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Platform setup and tracking.
  • Program terms and commission structure.
  • Partner recruitment plan.
  • Creative assets for partners.
  • Fraud monitoring.
  • Reporting on sales and costs.
  • Management hours and pricing.

Ask for a test purchase through an affiliate link before launch. It confirms that tracking, attribution and commission calculation work correctly.

Request clear partner guidelines on brand use, paid ads, coupon sites and claims. Rules protect your brand and help good partners promote you correctly.

Ask the expert about programs they have managed: how many active partners they had, which types performed best and how they handled rule breaking. Practical stories show whether they focus on quality partners or just on signing up as many sites as possible.

How to brief an affiliate expert

  1. Products and margins.
  2. Store platform.
  3. Target partners: bloggers, creators, review sites.
  4. Budget for commissions and tools.
  5. Brand rules.
  6. Return and refund rates.
  7. Goals and timeline.

Decide which partners fit your brand. A few trusted creators and niche sites often bring better customers than large numbers of coupon or cashback sites.

Set rules for paid search and brand terms. Some partners bid on your brand name in ads, which can raise costs without bringing new customers.

Prepare useful assets: product photos, honest descriptions, comparison points and landing pages. Good material helps partners create content that converts.

Require clear disclosure and honest claims. Misleading promotions damage trust and may break consumer protection rules.

Consider which customers count as new. Many programs pay higher commissions for first-time buyers and lower or none for existing customers. Clear rules prevent paying partners for sales that would have happened anyway through your own channels.

What drives the price

  • platform and tools
  • number of partners
  • recruitment effort
  • creative assets
  • fraud monitoring
  • ongoing management

Setting up a program on existing software costs much less than ongoing recruitment and management of many partners across several countries.

Platform fees and commissions are separate from the expert's fee.

Red flags

  • No test of tracking.
  • Unclear terms and rules.
  • Recruitment of low-quality or coupon-only sites without discussion.
  • No fraud monitoring.
  • No disclosure requirements.

Review partner performance monthly. Reward partners who bring real customers, and remove those who break the rules.

Keep communication with partners regular, such as a short monthly update on new products and offers.

Plan partner payments carefully. Pay commissions after the return window closes, keep clear records of each sale and communicate payment dates. Reliable, transparent payments are one of the strongest reasons good partners stay with a program.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • Tracking was tested with a purchase.
  • Terms and commission rates are clear.
  • Partner rules cover ads and claims.
  • Disclosure is required.
  • Fraud monitoring is in place.
