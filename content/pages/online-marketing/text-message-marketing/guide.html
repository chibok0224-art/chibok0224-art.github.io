What a good SMS marketing package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Platform setup and integration.
  • Sign-up forms with clear consent wording.
  • Automated flows such as welcome or cart reminders.
  • Campaign messages written and scheduled.
  • Opt-out handling and compliance basics.
  • Tracking of clicks and sales.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

Ask to see every message before it goes out, including automated ones. A typo or wrong link in a text reaches customers instantly and cannot be edited after sending.

Ask which countries the expert has run text campaigns in. Rules on consent, quiet hours and required wording vary by country and sometimes by region. An expert who knows your market will set up forms and messages correctly from the start.

How to brief an SMS marketing expert

  1. Your business and customers.
  2. Countries you send to.
  3. Platform and store or CRM you use.
  4. Goals: sales, bookings or reminders.
  5. How you collect numbers and consent today.
  6. Brand voice and offers you plan.
  7. Budget, including sending costs.

Remember that sending texts costs money per message, separate from the expert's fee. Ask for an estimate based on your list size and planned frequency.

Start with automated messages that customers expect, such as order updates or cart reminders. They are useful to customers and usually perform better than broad promotions.

Write messages for small screens and busy moments. One clear idea, a short link and a reason to act now work better than long texts with several offers. Ask the expert to keep a library of approved messages you can reuse for future campaigns and seasonal events.

Think about timing as well as content. Texts sent late at night or during busy work hours can annoy people. Ask the expert to schedule messages for sensible local times and to respect any quiet hours that apply in the countries you send to.

What drives the price

  • platform setup and integrations
  • number of flows and campaigns
  • message writing
  • list growth tools
  • reporting
  • multiple countries or languages

Setting up a few automated flows costs less than managing ongoing campaigns with testing and monthly reports.

Red flags

  • Offers to sell or provide phone lists.
  • No mention of consent or opt-outs.
  • Plans for very frequent promotional texts.
  • Messages without your brand name.
  • No tracking of results.

Keep records of how each person gave consent. If a question about permission ever comes up, clear records protect your business.

Tips for a smoother project

Review results after the first few campaigns. Look at clicks, sales, replies and especially opt-out rates. A rising number of opt-outs is an early warning that messages are too frequent or not useful enough, and it is better to adjust early than to lose subscribers.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • Every subscriber gave clear consent.
  • Opt-out instructions are in every message.
  • I approved all messages before sending.
  • I know the sending costs.
  • Clicks and sales will be tracked.
