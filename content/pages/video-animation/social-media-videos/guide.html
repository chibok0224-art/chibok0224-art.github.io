What a good social media video package includes

Short-form video gigs are usually priced per video or in monthly bundles. Check that the package states:

  • Number and length of videos.
  • Format: vertical 9:16, and any square or horizontal versions.
  • Captions: style, accuracy and language.
  • Hooks: an attention-grabbing opening, often with on-screen text.
  • Music and sound effects, licensed for business use.
  • Graphics: logo, lower thirds, zooms and transitions.
  • Revisions, turnaround and file delivery.

How to brief a social media video editor

  1. Your footage, organized in a shared folder, with the best takes marked.
  2. The platforms you post on.
  3. The goal of each video: teach, entertain, show a product, drive a click.
  4. Videos you like, from your niche, with notes on pacing and captions.
  5. Brand elements: fonts, colors, logo and any intro or outro.
  6. The first line or hook you want, if you have one.
  7. Your posting schedule, so delivery can be planned.

Film with editing in mind: hold the phone vertically, record a few seconds before and after each take, and film in good light. Clean, well-lit footage leaves the editor time for the creative work instead of repairs.

Review your analytics every few weeks with the editor. Which videos kept people watching past the first few seconds? Share what worked, so the next batch builds on it.

What drives the price

  • number and length of videos
  • editing style: simple cuts versus heavy motion graphics
  • captions, translations and sound design
  • repurposing long videos, including picking the clips
  • stock footage and licensed music
  • turnaround and monthly volume

Red flags

  • Popular songs used on business videos with no license.
  • Guaranteed views, followers or "viral" videos.
  • A portfolio of horizontal videos only.
  • Auto-generated captions with obvious errors left in.
  • Requests for your social media password to post for you.

Build a simple content plan with the editor rather than ordering one video at a time. A monthly list of topics, with a mix of formats such as tips, behind-the-scenes clips and product moments, makes filming easier and keeps your posting steady. It also lets the editor batch similar videos, which usually means faster turnaround and a more consistent look across your feed. Review the plan together each month and drop the formats that viewers skip.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My footage is organized and filmed vertically.
  • I have listed platforms, goals and example videos.
  • Music is licensed for business use.
  • Captions and a hook are part of each video.
  • I know the turnaround and my posting schedule fits it.
