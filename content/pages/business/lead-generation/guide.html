What a good lead generation package includes

Lead generation gigs are usually priced by the number of leads and the research needed. Check that the package states:

  • Number of leads and the criteria they must meet.
  • Fields: name, job title, company, website, email, phone, location, source link.
  • Sources: how and where the leads are found.
  • Verification of emails and details.
  • Freshness: researched for you, or from an existing database.
  • Format: spreadsheet or import into your CRM.
  • Replacement policy for invalid leads.

How to brief a lead generation expert

  1. Your ideal customer: industry, company size, location and job titles.
  2. Exclusions: current customers, competitors, regions you do not serve.
  3. Fields you need, in a sample spreadsheet.
  4. How you will use the leads: email, calls, LinkedIn or ads.
  5. Quality over quantity: tell the expert which matters more for you.
  6. A small test: a first batch you can check before the full order.

Check a sample of leads yourself: open the company website, look up the person's role, and send a few emails. It quickly shows whether the list is accurate and whether the criteria were understood.

Plan your first message before the leads arrive. A short, relevant note that shows you understand the company's situation gets better replies than a generic pitch, and it protects your reputation with people who have never heard of you.

What drives the price

  • number of leads
  • how specific and hard to find the criteria are
  • manual research versus database exports
  • email verification and extra fields such as phone numbers
  • CRM import and outreach setup
  • the expert's experience in your industry

Red flags

  • Huge numbers of leads for a very low price, often old or scraped data.
  • No explanation of sources or verification.
  • Lists of personal emails when you asked for business contacts.
  • Offers to send bulk email from their accounts on your behalf with no consent checks.
  • No replacement for bounced or invalid leads.

Data protection and marketing rules differ by country. Check what applies before you contact leads, especially individuals and people in regions with strict consent rules.

Track what happens to each batch of leads: how many emails bounced, how many replied and how many became customers. Share the results with the expert. It helps them refine the criteria for the next batch, and it tells you which sources and segments are worth paying for again and which ones to drop from the next order.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have defined my ideal customer and exclusions.
  • I have a sample spreadsheet with the fields I need.
  • The expert has explained sources and verification.
  • I will test a small batch first.
  • I have checked the rules for contacting these leads.
