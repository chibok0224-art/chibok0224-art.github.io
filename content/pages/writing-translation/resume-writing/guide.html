What a good resume package includes

Resume gigs are usually priced by career level and by what is included. Check that the package states:

  • Documents: resume, cover letter, LinkedIn profile, or a combination.
  • Writing or editing: a full rewrite, or improving your existing draft.
  • Targeting: tailored to a specific role or industry.
  • ATS-friendly layout with standard headings.
  • Input process: questionnaire, call or messages.
  • File formats: editable document and PDF.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

What to send a resume writer

  1. Your current resume, even if it is out of date.
  2. Two or three job ads you want to apply for.
  3. Achievements with evidence: projects, results, awards, promotions. Include numbers only when you can support them.
  4. Your career story: why you are moving, and any gaps you want explained.
  5. The country you are applying in, since resume conventions differ.
  6. Things to leave out, such as old roles or personal details.

Read the finished resume as if you were in an interview. Every line should be something you can explain with an example. Remove anything that is exaggerated or that you do not recognize as your own work.

Keep the editable file and update it after every project or promotion. It is much easier to tailor a current resume for a new application than to reconstruct a year of work from memory.

What drives the price

  • career level, from entry level to executive
  • full rewrite versus editing
  • number of documents: cover letter, LinkedIn profile, extra versions
  • specialist fields such as academic, technical or federal resumes
  • calls or interviews included
  • rush delivery

Red flags

  • Guarantees of interviews or job offers.
  • No questions about your experience before writing.
  • Samples full of generic phrases that could describe anyone.
  • Offers to invent experience, qualifications or results.
  • Designs with graphics and columns that screening software may not read.

Tailor the resume for each application, even slightly. Reorder bullet points so the most relevant experience comes first, and mirror the key skills from the job ad where they are true for you. A resume that clearly matches the role is easier for both screening software and busy recruiters to say yes to. Keep a list of the versions you send, so you know which one each employer received when they call you back.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have job ads that show the roles I want.
  • I have listed my real achievements with evidence.
  • The package states the documents, layout and revisions.
  • I will get an editable file and a PDF.
  • I can explain every line in an interview.
