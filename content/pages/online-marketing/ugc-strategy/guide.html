What a good UGC strategy package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Audience and angle research.
  • Content angles and hooks to test.
  • Creator profiles.
  • Brief templates.
  • Testing plan and metrics.
  • Usage rights guidance.
  • Deliverables, timeline and support.

Ask for ready-to-use brief templates. Clear briefs save time with every creator and lead to more consistent content.

Request a simple testing framework: which variables to test first, how long to run each test and what results mean a video is worth scaling.

Ask the strategist for examples of angles they discovered and how those angles performed. A strong strategist can explain why a certain hook worked for a particular audience, which shows they understand customers rather than only trends.

How to brief a UGC strategist

  1. Product and benefits.
  2. Customers and their objections.
  3. Current content and results.
  4. Channels: ads, social, website.
  5. Budget for creators and ads.
  6. Brand rules and claims.
  7. Goals and timeline.

Share customer reviews and support questions. Real words from customers are the best source of hooks and angles, and they make creator videos sound authentic.

Define claims creators can and cannot make. Honest, approved key points protect your brand and keep ads compliant with platform rules.

Plan for volume. UGC works through testing many variations, so the strategy should include a realistic production rhythm and budget.

Track results by angle, not only by video. Knowing which angles work lets you brief future creators far more effectively.

Think about where each video will live after testing. A winning ad might also work on product pages, in emails or on social profiles. Planning these uses upfront ensures you request the right rights and formats from creators from the beginning.

What drives the price

  • research depth
  • number of angles and briefs
  • creator sourcing
  • testing setup
  • reporting
  • ongoing support

A strategy document with briefs costs much less than an ongoing program with creator sourcing, testing and monthly reporting.

Creator fees and ad spend are separate from the strategist's fee.

Red flags

  • Vague briefs.
  • No testing plan.
  • Unclear usage rights.
  • Exaggerated claims in scripts.
  • No measurement by angle.

Review results monthly and retire angles that no longer perform. Audiences tire of the same messages.

Keep a library of winning hooks and videos with their rights and expiry dates.

Decide early who manages creators day to day: sending products, answering questions, collecting drafts and handling payments. Clear ownership prevents delays and keeps creator relationships positive, which matters when you want to reorder from the best performers.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I shared reviews and customer questions.
  • Briefs are ready to use.
  • A testing plan exists.
  • Usage rights are defined.
  • Results are tracked by angle.
