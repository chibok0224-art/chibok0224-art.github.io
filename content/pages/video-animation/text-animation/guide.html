What a good text animation package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Video length and number of words or lines.
  • Audio sync, if needed.
  • Style and transitions.
  • Fonts and colors, with licenses.
  • Formats and sizes.
  • Revisions.
  • Delivery time.

Ask for a short style test of a few seconds before the full video. It confirms the fonts, motion and pace you like, and changes at this stage are quick.

Request a version without background or music if you might reuse the text elsewhere, such as over other footage or in a different language edit.

Watch the designer's samples at normal speed on a phone. Text that looks smooth on a large monitor can feel rushed or cramped on a small screen. Samples that stay readable on a phone show the designer understands pacing and sizing for real viewers.

How to brief a text animator

  1. The final text, proofread.
  2. Final audio or music, if syncing.
  3. Purpose and platform.
  4. Brand fonts and colors.
  5. Reference videos you like.
  6. Sizes and lengths needed.
  7. Deadline.

Proofread the text carefully before sending. Every word change after animation means retiming, and spelling mistakes are very visible when words fill the screen.

Mark the most important words. Animators can make key words larger, bolder or slower, which guides the viewer's eye and makes the message easier to remember.

Think about where viewers will watch. Mobile viewers need bigger text and fewer words per screen than people watching on a large display.

What drives the price

  • video length
  • amount of text
  • audio sync complexity
  • custom illustrations or backgrounds
  • number of formats
  • rush delivery

A short animated quote costs much less than a full lyric video with precise syncing, custom backgrounds and several formats.

Red flags

  • Text that moves too fast to read.
  • Unlicensed fonts.
  • Poor sync with the voice or music.
  • No style test before full production.
  • Spelling mistakes in samples.

Watch the final video without sound. If the message is still clear, the text animation is doing its job for viewers who scroll with audio off.

Tips for a smoother project

Keep a list of the fonts, colors and motion styles used. It makes future videos consistent and quicker to produce, even with a different animator.

Plan the video's ending. Many text animations finish with a call to action, a website or a logo. Make sure that final frame stays on screen long enough and that the important details are large enough to read before the video loops or ends.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My text is final and proofread.
  • I sent the final audio for syncing.
  • Fonts are licensed for video.
  • I will approve a style test first.
  • Each line stays on screen long enough to read.
