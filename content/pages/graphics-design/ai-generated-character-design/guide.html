What a good AI character package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Character concept and variations.
  • Consistency across poses.
  • Character sheet.
  • Editing to fix flaws.
  • Resolution and formats.
  • Usage rights and tool terms.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

Ask for the same character in several different poses and settings before final approval. Consistency is the real test of AI character work.

Request a character sheet with colors, proportions and key details. It helps any future artist, animator or AI workflow keep the character recognizable.

Check hands, eyes and accessories carefully. AI often changes small details between images, which breaks consistency.

Ask the designer how they keep characters consistent: reference images, fixed settings, editing or redrawing. A clear answer shows a reliable process rather than luck. Ask to see a past character used across many images over time.

How to brief an AI character designer

  1. Purpose: mascot, story, game, avatar.
  2. Personality and backstory.
  3. Look: age, style, clothing, colors.
  4. References that are not famous characters.
  5. Poses and scenes needed.
  6. Formats and sizes.
  7. Usage and deadline.

Describe personality and story, not only looks. A cheerful helper, a wise guide and a mischievous sidekick need different expressions and poses.

Decide which details must never change, such as hair color, a scarf or a logo badge. Listing them helps the designer protect consistency.

Think about where the character will appear, from tiny app icons to large posters. Simple shapes and strong silhouettes work best at every size.

Be open about AI use where your audience or platform expects it.

Think about how the character will speak to your audience. A mascot with a clear name, personality traits and typical expressions is easier to use in posts, packaging and campaigns than a character that only exists as a picture.

What drives the price

  • number of poses and scenes
  • editing and cleanup
  • character sheet
  • resolution for print
  • commercial rights
  • rush delivery

A single character portrait costs much less than a full character pack with many poses, expressions and a detailed sheet.

Converting AI characters into clean vector or animation-ready art is usually a separate service.

Red flags

  • Characters that change between images.
  • Resemblance to famous characters.
  • Unclear commercial rights.
  • Unedited flaws.
  • No character sheet.

Store the approved images, character sheet and notes together. They are the reference for every future use of the character.

Review new images against the sheet before publishing.

Plan for future needs, such as new outfits, seasonal versions or merchandise. A character built with a clear sheet and clean source images is much easier to extend than one that exists only as a few lucky AI generations.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • The same character appears in several poses.
  • I will get a character sheet.
  • Key details are protected.
  • The character is original.
  • Commercial rights are clear.
