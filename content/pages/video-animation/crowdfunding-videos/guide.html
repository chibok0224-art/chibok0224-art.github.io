What a good crowdfunding video package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Video length and versions.
  • Script help or writing.
  • Filming or editing of your footage.
  • Animation or graphics.
  • Music with licenses.
  • Subtitles and formats for social media.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

Ask for short cut-down versions for social media and ads. Your campaign will need them to drive traffic, and cutting them from the same footage is efficient.

Plan the video to be ready before the campaign launches, with time to show it to a few trusted people. Their reactions reveal unclear parts while you can still fix them.

Watch campaign videos the maker has worked on, and look at how those campaigns were presented as a whole. Videos that clearly show a product, a team and a reason to back now are worth more than stylish videos that never explain what the project is.

How to brief a crowdfunding video maker

  1. Your project and the problem it solves.
  2. Who the backers are.
  3. The prototype and what is finished.
  4. Your story and team.
  5. Rewards and campaign goal.
  6. Existing footage and photos.
  7. Launch date.

Lead with the problem and the product in action. People decide quickly whether a project interests them, so the first seconds should show what it is and why it matters.

Be honest about what is finished and what is still in development. Clear, realistic videos build trust, reduce support questions and protect your reputation if timelines change.

Ask the video maker to include a clear call to action at the end: back the project, choose a reward, share the campaign. Viewers who are convinced should know exactly what to do next.

Make sure captions are added. Many people watch campaign pages and social clips without sound, so clear subtitles keep your message understandable everywhere. Ask for caption files as well as burned-in text, so you can use them on different platforms.

What drives the price

  • video length
  • filming days and locations
  • animation and graphics
  • script writing
  • music licensing
  • extra versions and subtitles

Editing footage you already have costs much less than a full production with filming, animation and a scripted story.

Red flags

  • Renders shown as real products without labels.
  • Unlicensed music.
  • No script or story planning.
  • No social media versions.
  • Promises of guaranteed funding.

Prepare the rest of the campaign too. A great video works best with a clear page, good photos and a plan to reach backers before launch.

Test the video with people who match your backers before launch. Ask them to describe the project in one sentence after watching. If their answers differ from your intended message, adjust the opening or script while there is still time.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • The video shows the real prototype.
  • Concepts are clearly labeled.
  • Music is licensed.
  • I will get social media versions.
  • The video is ready before launch.
