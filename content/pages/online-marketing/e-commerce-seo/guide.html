What a good e-commerce SEO package includes

E-commerce SEO gigs are sold as audits, one-time fixes or monthly work. Whichever you choose, the package should state:

  • Platform: Shopify, WooCommerce or another, and the specialist's experience with it.
  • Audit: a list of technical issues found, ranked by importance.
  • Product and category pages: how many will be optimized, and what that means.
  • Keyword research for categories and top products.
  • Technical fixes: duplicate pages from filters, broken links, redirects, speed and structured data.
  • Who makes the changes: the specialist, your developer, or you from their instructions.
  • Reporting: which numbers you will see, such as search traffic, rankings and revenue from search.

What to prepare before you order

  1. Your store address and platform.
  2. Access: staff access to your store, plus search console and analytics access.
  3. Priority products and categories: best sellers and the ones with the best margins.
  4. Competitors that rank well for your products.
  5. Markets and languages you sell in.
  6. Recent changes, such as a platform move, redesign or large price change.
  7. Limits: pages or settings that must not be changed.

If you do not have a search console account yet, set one up in your own name before ordering. It shows how search engines see your store and lets you check the specialist's work.

Ask how out-of-stock and discontinued products will be handled. Deleting product pages can throw away search traffic and create broken links, while keeping them without a plan frustrates visitors. A good specialist will suggest a clear rule, such as showing alternatives or redirecting to the closest category.

What drives the price

  • number of products, categories and languages
  • audit only versus audit and implementation
  • rewriting product and category descriptions
  • technical fixes that need a developer
  • migrations and redirects from an old store
  • one-time work versus ongoing monthly work

Red flags

  • Guaranteed first-page or number-one rankings.
  • Offers to buy links in bulk or use private link networks.
  • Plans to auto-generate thousands of thin pages.
  • No mention of your store platform or its known search issues.
  • Reports with rankings for phrases nobody searches, and no sales data.

Judge the work by search traffic and sales from search over several months, not by a single ranking on a single day.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have search console and analytics set up in my own name.
  • I have listed my priority products and categories.
  • The package states whether the specialist or I make the changes.
  • The specialist has experience with my store platform.
  • No guaranteed rankings and no bought links.
