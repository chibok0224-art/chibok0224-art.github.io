What a good Stable Diffusion package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Number of images.
  • Model and license.
  • Style approach.
  • Editing.
  • Resolution.
  • Usage rights.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

Ask which base model and any custom styles are used, and confirm their licenses allow your use.

Request drafts before final images. Choosing a direction early improves consistency.

Look at the artist's portfolio for consistency across sets, not only single images. Series of images with the same character, product or style show real control over the tools, which is the main advantage of Stable Diffusion workflows.

How to brief a Stable Diffusion artist

  1. Purpose.
  2. Subject and scenes.
  3. Reference images you own.
  4. Style and colors.
  5. Sizes.
  6. Text to add later.
  7. Usage and deadline.

Provide your own reference images and brand assets. Avoid asking for imitations of living artists or copyrighted characters.

Plan text separately in design software, since AI images often render text poorly.

Be transparent about AI use where your audience or platforms expect it.

Ask how the artist handles your files and any custom styles trained for you. Brand references and trained styles are valuable assets, and you should agree on who keeps them and whether they can be reused for other clients.

Discuss how the artist will keep a character or product consistent across many images, such as reference sheets, fixed settings or custom styles. A reliable process matters when you need images over months, not only for one campaign.

What drives the price

  • number of images
  • custom style setup
  • editing
  • upscaling
  • commercial rights
  • rush delivery

A few draft images cost much less than a custom-trained style with many edited, high-resolution images.

Red flags

  • Unclear model licenses.
  • Styles copied from living artists.
  • Unedited flaws.
  • Training on images without permission.
  • No usage rights.

Keep a record of models, settings and edits used for each image.

Tips for a smoother project

Consider how images will be used across formats, such as social posts, banners and print. Planning sizes and compositions in advance avoids awkward crops.

Think about disclosure and audience expectations. Some customers and platforms prefer to know when images are AI-generated, and being open about it can protect trust in your brand.

Keep final images with their edit files so future variations match the originals.

Ask about hardware and turnaround times. Stable Diffusion work can involve many generations and upscaling steps, and artists with capable equipment and a clear workflow can deliver revisions faster, which matters when you need images for a campaign deadline.
