What a good UGC ad package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Number of videos and lengths.
  • Hooks and variations included.
  • Script: written by you, the creator or together.
  • Editing: captions, music and cuts.
  • Format: vertical, square or both.
  • Usage rights for paid ads, with duration.
  • Raw footage, revisions and delivery time.

Ask for raw footage as well as the edited ad. Your team or editor can then create new cuts for testing without another shoot.

Watch the creator's previous ads with the sound off as well as on. Many people scroll with muted audio, so clear captions, visible product shots and readable text on screen often matter as much as what the creator says.

How to brief a UGC ad creator

  1. The product and who it is for.
  2. Key benefits and approved claims.
  3. The ad goal: clicks, sales or app installs.
  4. Hook ideas and the main message.
  5. Platforms and formats.
  6. Examples of ads you like.
  7. Usage rights and deadline.

The first seconds decide whether people keep watching. Ask for several different openings, such as a problem, a surprising result or a quick demonstration, and test which works best.

Let creators speak in their own words within your key points. Natural delivery is the reason UGC works; a stiff, fully scripted read often looks like a traditional ad.

Choose creators who match your real customers. Age, lifestyle, tone of voice and how they naturally use products affect whether viewers trust the ad. A creator who would genuinely use your product usually makes a more convincing video than one with a larger following.

What drives the price

  • number of videos and variations
  • script writing
  • editing and captions
  • length of usage rights
  • raw footage
  • creator audience and experience

Longer paid usage rights and exclusive deals cost more. Start with the rights you need for a test period and extend them if an ad performs well.

Red flags

  • No clear usage rights for paid ads.
  • Exaggerated or unproven claims in the script.
  • Only one version with no hook variations.
  • Poor sound or lighting in samples.
  • No disclosure when the platform requires it.

Follow advertising rules for disclosures and claims. Honest ads protect your ad account and your reputation with customers.

Plan how you will judge results before the ads go live. Decide which numbers matter, such as cost per purchase or click rate, and how long each version runs. Clear testing rules help you reorder from the creators whose videos actually perform.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • Paid usage rights are agreed in writing.
  • I approved the key points and claims.
  • I will get several hooks or versions.
  • I will receive raw footage.
  • The product will arrive in time for filming.
