What a good search ads strategy consultation includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Account audit.
  • Keyword plan and negatives.
  • Campaign structure.
  • Budget plan.
  • Tracking review.
  • Landing page notes.
  • Action list and review session.

Ask for a prioritized action list from the audit, with estimated impact. Fixing a few big problems often saves more than many small tweaks.

Request a check of conversion tracking. Decisions based on broken tracking waste money.

How to brief a search ads strategy consultant

  1. Business and offers.
  2. Margins and customer value.
  3. Current campaigns.
  4. Analytics and tracking access.
  5. Markets and languages.
  6. Budget.
  7. Goals.

Share your margins and customer lifetime value. Paid search only makes sense when the cost per customer is below what a customer is worth to you.

List your best-selling products or services. Starting with proven offers usually brings faster, clearer results than testing everything at once.

Mention competitors who bid on your brand name or category. The consultant can plan defensive and competitive strategies.

Ask consultants for examples of audits and what changed after recommendations were applied. Clear before-and-after results show real value.

Discuss how paid search fits with your other channels, such as SEO and email. Bidding heavily on searches where you already rank first organically may waste money, while combining channels well can lower overall costs and raise total sales over time.

What drives the price

  • session length and number
  • research and audit depth
  • written plan or report
  • number of channels or systems
  • follow-up support
  • consultant experience

A one-time audit costs much less than a full strategy with keyword research, structure rebuild and ongoing reviews.

Plan landing page improvements together with ads. Better pages often lower costs more than keyword changes.

Ask for a simple dashboard or report template so you can follow results yourself after the consultation ends.

Ask how the consultant thinks about match types, negative keywords and search terms reports. Clear explanations of these basics show they can control where your ads appear and stop paying for irrelevant searches, which is where most wasted budget hides.

Red flags

  • No questions about margins.
  • Clicks as the main success measure.
  • Requests for passwords or account ownership.
  • No tracking check.
  • Guaranteed rankings or sales.

Keep accounts in your name and review results monthly against profit.

For running ads, see our search ads guide. For pages, read our conversion rate guide and landing page design guide.

Agree on a review after the first month of changes to confirm improvements.

Keep a change log so you know which changes affected results.

Consider seasonality in the budget plan. Search demand changes through the year, and planning budget shifts in advance avoids overspending in quiet months or missing busy ones.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • Tracking is checked.
  • Margins are shared.
  • Accounts stay in my name.
  • The action list is prioritized.
  • Success means profit, not clicks.
