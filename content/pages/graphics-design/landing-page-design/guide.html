What a good landing page package includes

Landing page gigs range from a design mockup to a finished, published page. Before ordering, check that the package states:

  • Design only, or design and build, and in which tool.
  • Sections: how many, such as headline, benefits, proof, questions and a final call to action.
  • Mobile layout, designed and tested on a phone.
  • Copywriting: included, edited from your draft, or not included.
  • Forms and buttons connected to your email tool, store or booking system.
  • Tracking: analytics and ad pixels installed and tested.
  • Speed: compressed images and a light layout.
  • Revisions and delivery time.

How to brief a landing page designer

  1. One goal: the single action the visitor should take.
  2. Where visitors come from: an ad, an email, a social post or search. The page should match what they just saw.
  3. Your offer: what the visitor gets, the price or discount, and any deadline.
  4. Proof you can use: real reviews, logos of clients or numbers you can back up.
  5. Objections: the questions and doubts customers usually have before buying.
  6. Brand files and product photos.
  7. Pages you like, with notes on why.

Only use reviews and numbers that are real and that you have permission to show. Invented testimonials damage trust and may break advertising rules.

Ask the designer to keep the most important content in the first screen on a phone: what you offer, why it matters, and the button. Visitors from ads decide quickly, and many never scroll. Everything below that should answer questions and remove doubts, not repeat the headline.

What drives the price

  • design only versus design and build
  • number of sections and custom graphics
  • copywriting included or not
  • connections to forms, payments, booking or email tools
  • tracking setup and extra versions for testing
  • the designer's experience with pages for paid traffic, and rush delivery

Red flags

  • A portfolio of pretty pages with no mention of what they were built to achieve.
  • No mobile version, or a mobile version that is simply the desktop page shrunk down.
  • Several different buttons and goals on one page.
  • Offers to add fake reviews, fake countdown timers or invented numbers.
  • Large images and animations that make the page slow on a phone.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My page has one goal and one main button.
  • I know where visitors will come from.
  • The package states the tool, mobile layout and who writes the text.
  • Tracking will be set up and tested before launch.
  • All reviews and numbers on the page are real.
