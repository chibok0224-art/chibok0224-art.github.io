What a good vector tracing package includes

Check that the offer states:

  • Manual tracing, not only auto trace.
  • File formats: AI, EPS, SVG, PDF and PNG.
  • Color accuracy and color codes for print and web.
  • Text handling: matched font or outlines.
  • Transparent background versions.
  • Revisions to fix details.
  • Delivery time.

Zoom in on the finished file. Clean vector work has smooth curves and few anchor points, with no gaps, jagged edges or overlapping shapes.

Ask for print color codes as well as screen colors if you plan to print. Colors look different on screens and paper, so having both avoids surprises at the printer.

Check samples at large sizes, not only as small thumbnails. Problems like wobbly edges, uneven line widths and stray points only show when a file is enlarged, which is exactly what happens on signs, banners and vehicle graphics.

How to brief a vector tracing designer

  1. The best image you have, in the highest resolution.
  2. What it is for: print, signs, embroidery or web.
  3. Exact colors, if you know them.
  4. Font name, if known.
  5. Details to keep or simplify.
  6. File formats you need.
  7. Deadline.

Tell the designer about the final use. Embroidery, vinyl cutting and screen printing have special needs, such as fewer colors or no tiny details, and the file can be prepared for them.

If the original has mistakes or uneven lines, decide whether to keep them for faithfulness or clean them up. A short note avoids a result that is either too rough or too changed.

What drives the price

  • complexity of the image and details
  • number of colors
  • text and font matching
  • cleanup of a low-quality source
  • special preparation for print or cutting
  • fast delivery

A simple two-color logo costs much less than a detailed illustration, a photo-like image or a complex emblem with many small elements.

Red flags

  • Auto-traced files sold as manual work.
  • Bumpy curves and too many anchor points.
  • Colors that do not match the original.
  • Only a PNG delivered instead of vector files.
  • Fonts used without a license.

Keep your vector files safe in more than one place. Printers, sign makers and designers will ask for them again and again over the years.

Before sending the file to a printer or sign maker, ask them which format and color settings they prefer. Sharing those requirements with the tracing designer up front avoids a second round of conversion work later.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I sent the highest quality image I have.
  • The tracing is done by hand.
  • I will receive AI, EPS, SVG, PDF and PNG files.
  • Colors and fonts are matched and licensed.
  • The file is prepared for my final use.
