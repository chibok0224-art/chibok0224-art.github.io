What a good design package includes

Online design gigs range from a room layout to full-home concepts. Check that the package states:

  • Scope: which rooms or areas.
  • Deliverables: floor plan, layout, mood board, 3D renders.
  • Revisions and number of concepts.
  • Shopping list, if included.
  • File formats: PDF, images, CAD files.
  • Limits: concept design, not construction documents.
  • Delivery time.

Ask for a measured floor plan as the first step. If the plan does not match your space, everything built on it will be wrong, so confirm measurements before any furniture or renders are placed.

How to brief an online designer

  1. Measurements of every wall, window and door.
  2. Photos and video of the space.
  3. How you use the space and who uses it.
  4. Style references and things you dislike.
  5. Items to keep, with their sizes.
  6. Budget for furniture and works.
  7. Local constraints: rental rules, building rules.

Measure twice and include fixed features: outlets, switches, radiators, pipes and beams. Small details often decide where furniture and fittings can actually go.

Share a realistic budget. Designs that look amazing in renders but cost far more than you can spend are not useful. A designer who knows your budget can choose realistic furniture and finishes.

Treat renders as a guide. Real materials, light and colors look different from 3D images, so order samples of key finishes before committing.

Ask whether the designer can provide a simple list of measurements and product dimensions for contractors and delivery teams. Doors, stairs and lifts limit what furniture can be brought into a space, and checking sizes before ordering avoids pieces that look perfect in a render but cannot physically fit through the door or around a corner.

What drives the price

  • number of rooms and size of the space
  • 3D renders and their detail
  • number of concepts and revisions
  • shopping lists and sourcing
  • CAD files
  • the designer's experience

Local contractors, permits and materials are separate costs. Use the online design to get clearer quotes from local professionals.

Red flags

  • Promises of permit-ready plans without local licensing.
  • Advice to remove walls without structural checks.
  • No request for measurements.
  • Shopping lists with items not available in your country.
  • Unclear revision limits.

Check building and safety rules where you live before any work. Electrical, plumbing, gas and structural work usually require licensed professionals.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have accurate measurements and photos.
  • I know the deliverables and revision limits.
  • I understand this is concept design, not construction plans.
  • My budget is shared.
  • I will check local rules and use licensed professionals for works.
