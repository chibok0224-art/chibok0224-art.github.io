What a good GoDaddy package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Task: build, fix, DNS, email or migration.
  • Access method.
  • Backups before changes.
  • DNS record changes documented.
  • Testing of site and email.
  • Handover notes.
  • Timeline and pricing.

Ask for a list of every DNS record before and after changes. If email or the website stops working, you can restore the previous settings quickly.

Request a backup of the site before edits or migrations, stored where you can access it.

Ask the expert about similar GoDaddy jobs they have done, such as email setup or site migrations. Experience with the platform's specific menus and products means faster work and fewer risky experiments on your live account.

Plan changes for a quiet time, such as evenings or weekends, especially for DNS updates and migrations. Some changes take time to spread across the internet, and timing them well reduces the impact on customers.

How to brief a GoDaddy expert

  1. The problem or goal.
  2. Products you use: builder, hosting, email.
  3. Domain names.
  4. Screenshots of errors.
  5. Other services connected to the domain.
  6. Access method.
  7. Deadline.

List every service using your domain, such as email, store, booking tools or verification records. DNS changes for one service can break another if these are unknown.

Enable two-step verification on your account and keep the domain in your name. Experts should never need to own your domain.

Ask how the expert will test email after DNS changes: sending and receiving from different providers and checking that messages do not land in spam. Email problems are often noticed late, when customers complain.

Ask whether the expert can explain each change in plain words. Knowing what was changed and why, such as a new SPF record to stop email landing in spam, helps you understand your setup and spot problems faster in the future.

What drives the price

  • task complexity
  • number of sites or domains
  • migration
  • email setup
  • design work
  • rush delivery

Fixing one DNS record costs much less than migrating several sites and email accounts.

Review renewal settings for your domain and hosting while the expert is working. Expired domains can take your website and email offline, and auto-renewal with a current payment method prevents surprises.

Red flags

  • Requests for your password.
  • No backups.
  • Undocumented DNS changes.
  • Domain moved to the expert's account.
  • No testing after changes.

Remove the expert's access when the work is done and review account security settings.

Ask for a short handover document listing products, settings and where backups are stored. It helps you or another expert continue without starting from zero.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I use delegated access, not my password.
  • A backup exists.
  • DNS records are documented.
  • Site and email are tested.
  • The domain stays in my account.
