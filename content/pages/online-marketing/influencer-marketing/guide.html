What a good influencer marketing package includes

Influencer marketing gigs range from a researched list of creators to a fully managed campaign. The package should state:

  • Scope: creator research only, outreach, or full campaign management.
  • Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or others.
  • Number of creators researched or contacted, and the size range of their audiences.
  • Vetting: how audience quality, engagement and brand fit are checked.
  • Contracts: whether terms with creators are written down, including usage rights for their content.
  • Content review: whether you approve posts before they go live.
  • Reporting: reach, clicks, codes used or sales, and when you receive it.

How to brief an influencer marketing expert

  1. The goal: awareness, sales, app installs or content you can reuse.
  2. Your customers: age, location, interests and the platforms they use.
  3. Your product, the offer, and whether you can ship samples to creators.
  4. Creators you like and ones you would never work with.
  5. Budget: the expert's fee and a separate creator budget.
  6. Rules: claims creators may not make, and required ad labels.
  7. Tracking: discount codes or links per creator, so results can be compared.

Smaller creators with an engaged, well-matched audience can work better than large accounts with a broad one. Ask the expert to explain why each creator on the list fits your customers.

Agree in writing what happens to each creator's content after the campaign. If you want to reuse a post on your own channels or run it as an ad, that usually needs the creator's permission and may cost extra. Settling it before the post is made avoids awkward conversations later.

What drives the price

  • research only versus full campaign management
  • number of creators and platforms
  • creator fees and product costs, paid separately
  • contracts and usage rights for creator content
  • tracking setup and depth of reporting
  • campaign length and the expert's experience in your market

Red flags

  • Guaranteed sales, views or "viral" results.
  • No explanation of how fake followers and low-quality audiences are checked.
  • Suggestions to skip ad labels on paid or gifted posts.
  • Creator fees hidden inside the expert's fee, so you cannot see who was paid what.
  • Reports that show follower counts but no clicks, codes or sales.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have one clear goal and know who my customers are.
  • The creator budget is separate from the expert's fee.
  • I will see how each creator's audience was checked.
  • Paid and gifted posts will be clearly labeled as ads.
  • Each creator has their own code or link so I can compare results.
