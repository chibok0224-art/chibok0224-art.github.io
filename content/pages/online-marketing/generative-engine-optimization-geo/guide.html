What a good GEO package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Audit of how AI tools describe you today.
  • Question list your customers ask.
  • Content plan and improvements.
  • Business information consistency across the web.
  • Technical checks: crawlability and structure.
  • Measurement and reporting.
  • Timeline and pricing.

Ask for a before snapshot: what several AI assistants say about your business and competitors for your key questions. It gives you a baseline to measure progress against.

Request a list of factual errors AI tools repeat about you, such as old prices or wrong locations. Fixing the sources of those errors is often the quickest win.

Make sure the plan also improves your site for human visitors. Content that genuinely helps customers tends to be what AI tools and search engines value too.

Ask the expert how they test AI answers and how often. Because responses vary between tools and sessions, a careful expert checks several assistants, repeats questions and looks for patterns rather than reporting a single lucky result as proof of success.

How to brief a GEO expert

  1. Your business and key offers.
  2. Customer questions you want to answer.
  3. Competitors that appear in AI answers.
  4. Website access and content tools.
  5. Brand facts: prices, locations, policies.
  6. Markets and languages.
  7. Budget and timeline.

Collect the real questions customers ask in emails, calls and reviews. These are the questions people also type or speak into AI assistants, and answering them clearly is the core of GEO.

Keep your business facts consistent everywhere: website, business listings, social profiles and directories. Conflicting information confuses both people and AI tools.

Invest in being mentioned by trusted sources, such as industry publications, partners and review sites. Genuine mentions build credibility that AI tools often rely on.

Avoid tricks like hidden text or mass-produced pages. They can harm your reputation and search visibility.

What drives the price

  • number of pages and questions
  • content creation
  • technical fixes
  • outreach for mentions
  • number of markets
  • reporting frequency

An audit with recommendations costs much less than ongoing content creation, outreach and monthly monitoring across several markets.

Content writing is often priced separately from strategy and audits.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed AI mentions or rankings.
  • Mass-produced low-quality content.
  • No baseline or measurement.
  • Ignores website basics.
  • Fake reviews or paid fake mentions.

Review AI answers about your brand regularly, such as monthly. Tools update often, and early detection of errors lets you correct sources quickly.

Keep improving content as your offers change. Outdated pages are a common source of wrong AI answers.
