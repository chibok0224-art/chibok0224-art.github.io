What a good email marketing package includes

Email marketing gigs cover platform setup, automated flows, one-off campaigns or monthly management. Whatever you choose, the package should state:

  • Platform: which email tool will be used, and whether setup or migration is included.
  • Deliverables: the number of emails, flows or campaigns.
  • Copy and design: whether the specialist writes and designs the emails, or you supply them.
  • Sign-up forms: pop-ups or embedded forms on your website, if included.
  • Deliverability checks: domain sending settings and a spam test.
  • Testing: test sends on phone and desktop before anything goes live.
  • Reporting: what numbers you will see and how often.

How to brief an email marketing expert

  1. Your goal: first purchases, repeat purchases, bookings or keeping in touch.
  2. Your list: how many subscribers, how they signed up, and when you last emailed them.
  3. Your platform and access to it, plus access to your website for forms.
  4. Brand files: logo, colors, fonts and a few product photos.
  5. Voice: two or three emails from other brands you like, with a note on why.
  6. Offers you can use, such as a welcome discount or free shipping, and ones you do not want to give.
  7. Rules: claims you cannot make and any legal text you must include.

A simple first project is a welcome series for new subscribers. It is easy to measure and keeps working after the order ends. If you sell online, add an abandoned-cart flow next.

What drives the price

  • number of emails, flows and campaigns
  • copywriting and custom design versus your own content
  • platform setup or migration from another tool
  • list cleanup and segmentation
  • connections to your store, booking tool or website
  • one-time setup versus ongoing monthly work

Red flags

  • The specialist offers to supply a list of email addresses.
  • No mention of consent, unsubscribe links or sending settings.
  • Promises of a fixed open rate or a guaranteed amount of sales.
  • They want your platform password instead of their own user account.
  • Samples that look good as images but were never sent or tested.

Email works best when people have a reason to sign up. A focused page for your offer helps; our landing page design guide covers that. Rules on consent and marketing email differ between countries, so check the rules where you and your subscribers live.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • Everyone on my list signed up to hear from me.
  • I know which platform will be used and I own the account.
  • The package states the number of emails and whether copy and design are included.
  • Sending settings and test sends are part of the job.
  • I know which numbers I will see in reports.
