What a good flyer design package includes

Flyer gigs are usually priced by the number of sides and concepts. Check that the package states:

  • Size: such as A5, A4, US Letter or a custom size.
  • Sides: single or double-sided.
  • Concepts and revision rounds.
  • Print-ready file: high-resolution PDF with bleed and correct color settings.
  • Digital versions for social media, email or your website.
  • Images: your photos or licensed stock.
  • Source file for future edits.

How to brief a flyer designer

  1. The purpose: an event, an offer, an opening, a service.
  2. The headline: the one message people should see first.
  3. Key details: date, time, place, price, contact, QR code link.
  4. The action: call, scan, visit, book.
  5. Audience and where the flyer will be seen.
  6. Brand files: logo, colors and fonts.
  7. Printer specifications: size, bleed and file format.

Proofread every detail yourself before approving: dates, phone numbers, web addresses and prices. Test the QR code with your phone. A single wrong digit on a printed run is expensive to fix.

Print one copy at home or order a single proof before the full run. Hold it at arm's length: if the headline and action are not clear in a few seconds, simplify the design before you print hundreds.

What drives the price

  • number of sides and size
  • number of concepts and revisions
  • custom illustration or photo editing
  • extra digital sizes
  • editable templates for reuse
  • rush delivery before an event

Red flags

  • The designer does not ask about print size or printer requirements.
  • Files delivered in low resolution or without bleed for printing.
  • Stock images with no license information.
  • A crowded layout with many fonts and no clear headline.
  • No source file offered, even at extra cost.

Think about where people will see the flyer and design for that moment. A flyer on a busy notice board needs a big, simple headline that stands out from across the room. A flyer handed out in person can carry a little more detail on the back. A flyer shared on social media needs to be readable on a small screen. Tell the designer the main place it will be used, so the layout, text size and contrast are chosen to match. If you print many copies, compare paper options with your printer as well.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have one headline, key details and one action.
  • I have my printer's size, bleed and file requirements.
  • I will get print and digital versions if I need both.
  • All images are mine or licensed.
  • I will proofread every detail and test the QR code.
