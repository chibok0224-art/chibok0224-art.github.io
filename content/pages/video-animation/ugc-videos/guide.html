What you actually get when you order

Two gigs with the same headline can deliver very different things. Before ordering, check that the package spells out each of these:

  • Number and length of videos: for example "1 video, 30–45 seconds". Some packages include several hooks (different opening lines) for the same video, which is valuable for ad testing.
  • Format: vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Ask for a 1:1 or 4:5 version if you also run feed ads.
  • Editing: captions, music, cuts and b-roll, or just raw clips for your own editor.
  • Raw footage: useful if you want to re-cut ads later. Often a paid extra.
  • Usage rights: organic posting only, or paid ads too, and for how long.
  • Delivery time: remember to add the time it takes your product to reach the creator.

How to brief a UGC creator

Most disappointing UGC comes from a vague brief. A good one fits on a single page:

  1. Who the video is for. One sentence about your customer and their problem.
  2. The one message. The single thing a viewer should remember. Not five features.
  3. Must-say and must-not-say. Required claims, brand name pronunciation, and anything legally off-limits.
  4. Two or three reference videos you like, with a note on what you like about each.
  5. Hook ideas. Suggest a few opening lines, but let the creator adapt them to their own voice.
  6. Technical specs. Aspect ratio, length, captions yes/no, and file format.

What drives the price

Prices range widely, so compare packages on what is included rather than the headline number. The main factors are:

  • number of videos and hook variations
  • whether editing, captions and raw footage are included
  • the scope and duration of usage rights, especially for paid ads
  • on-camera talent versus hands-only or voice-over formats
  • rush delivery

Red flags

  • A portfolio that is all stock-looking footage, or the same video shown in every gig.
  • No mention of usage rights anywhere in the gig or the seller's replies.
  • Recent reviews that mention missed deadlines, even if the overall rating is high.
  • Pressure to move payment or communication off the marketplace. That also removes your buyer protection.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have watched at least three portfolio videos from this seller.
  • The package states video count, length, format and revisions.
  • Usage rights for my intended use (organic or ads) are confirmed in writing.
  • I know whether I need to ship the product, and the delivery date allows for it.
  • My one-page brief is ready to send.
