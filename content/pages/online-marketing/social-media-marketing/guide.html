Strategy, content or management?

  • Strategy: a one-off plan covering audience, platforms, content themes, posting frequency and goals. Good if you will post yourself.
  • Content creation: a batch of designed posts, carousels or short videos with captions and hashtags, ready for you to schedule.
  • Management: someone plans, creates, schedules and often replies to comments every month.
  • Paid social: setting up and optimizing ads. Usually a separate service with its own budget.

What a monthly package should state

  • Platforms covered, for example Instagram and Facebook, or LinkedIn only.
  • Number of posts per week and the format (single image, carousel, Reel, story).
  • Who creates the visuals and captions, and whether stock images or your own photos are used.
  • Approval process: when you see the content calendar and how changes are handled.
  • Community management: whether replying to comments and messages is included.
  • Reporting: which numbers you get and how often.

What to share before starting

  1. Your audience: who they are and what they care about.
  2. Your goal: awareness, traffic to your site, leads, or sales.
  3. Your brand voice, with a few posts you like from any account.
  4. Brand assets: logo, colors, fonts, product photos.
  5. Topics that are off-limits and claims you cannot make.

What drives the price

  • number of platforms and posts per week
  • video content versus static images
  • community management and response times
  • custom photography or design versus templates and stock images
  • reporting depth and strategy calls

Red flags

  • Guaranteed follower, like or view numbers.
  • Requests for your password instead of a user role.
  • A portfolio of templates with no examples of real accounts they have grown.
  • Vague promises such as "boost your growth" with no stated deliverables.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I know whether I need strategy, content, management or ads.
  • The package states platforms, posts per week and formats.
  • I will approve a content calendar before posts go live.
  • Access will be granted through user roles, not my password.
  • We agreed on the numbers that define success.
