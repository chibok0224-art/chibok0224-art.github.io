What a good Shopify package includes

Shopify gigs range from installing a theme to a fully custom store design, plus migrations and fixes. A clear package states:

  • Theme: which theme will be used, and whether it is free or paid.
  • Pages: home, collection, product, cart, about, contact and policy pages.
  • Products: how many products and variants will be uploaded, if any.
  • Settings: payments, shipping zones, taxes, and email notifications.
  • Apps: which apps will be installed and which ones charge a monthly fee.
  • Mobile layout checked on real phones, since many shoppers buy on mobile.
  • Revisions, delivery time, and a short guide to managing the store.

How to brief a Shopify developer

Stores have many small settings, and each one missed in the brief becomes a question later. Send:

  1. What you sell and roughly how many products and variants (sizes, colors).
  2. Product data: a spreadsheet with titles, descriptions, prices and photos.
  3. Stores you like, with what you like about them.
  4. Where you ship and how you want to charge for shipping.
  5. Brand files: logo, colors and fonts. Our logo design guide explains which files to have.
  6. For migrations: your current platform and exactly what must be moved.
  7. Must-have features such as subscriptions, bundles, reviews or wholesale pricing.

What drives the price

  • a ready-made theme versus a custom design
  • number of products and variants to upload
  • migration from another platform, including redirects
  • custom features that need code or several apps
  • product photos and product descriptions, if the developer creates them
  • the developer's experience and how quickly you need the store

Red flags

  • The developer asks for your owner login instead of staff or collaborator access.
  • The developer wants the store created under their own account, to transfer later.
  • A long list of paid apps with no explanation of what each one does or costs.
  • A custom store promised in a day for a very low price. This usually means a theme with your logo.
  • The portfolio has no live stores you can browse on your phone.

Before you accept delivery, place a real test order on your phone: add a product to the cart, pay, and check the confirmation email and the shipping cost. Then refund it. This finds most setup mistakes while the developer is still working on your order.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • The store, domain and payment settings are in my name.
  • I have a product spreadsheet with photos ready.
  • I know which paid theme and apps will be used and what they cost each month.
  • I have decided where I ship and how I charge for shipping.
  • I have browsed at least three live stores from the developer's portfolio on my phone.
