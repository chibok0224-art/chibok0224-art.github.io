What a good photo editing service includes

Image editing gigs are usually priced per image and by the type of edit. Check that the service states:

  • Number of images.
  • Edits: background removal, color, retouching, cleanup, restoration.
  • Output: format, size, background and color profile.
  • Consistency across a set.
  • Revisions per image.
  • Turnaround and confidentiality for private photos.

Send one or two sample images before ordering a large batch. It shows the editor's quality on your actual photos and helps you agree on a style. Many editors offer a test edit at a small price for exactly this reason.

How to brief a photo editor

  1. The original files, in the best quality available.
  2. What to change for each image, with arrows or notes if needed.
  3. Where images will be used: store, print, social, website.
  4. Required sizes and backgrounds, for example white for marketplaces.
  5. A reference: an edited photo you like.
  6. What must stay true: product colors, logos, skin tones.

Compare edited product photos with the real product in daylight. If the color is off, customers will notice when the parcel arrives, and returns cost far more than a revision.

For large batches, agree on file naming and folder structure before work starts, for example by product code and angle. Clear names save hours when you upload to your store, and they make it easy to spot any missing or duplicated images once the batch comes back.

What drives the price

  • number of images
  • complexity: simple backgrounds versus hair, glass or fine detail
  • retouching depth
  • restoration of damaged photos
  • compositing several images
  • turnaround time

Ask how the editor handles tricky areas such as hair, fur, glass, jewelry and transparent packaging. These are where cheap background removal looks obviously fake. A couple of test edits on your hardest images shows whether the editor can handle them before you commit to a large order.

Red flags

  • Samples with rough edges around cutouts.
  • Colors that look very different from the originals.
  • Retouching that changes body shape or product size without being asked.
  • Very large batches promised very fast with no quality check.
  • No confidentiality for private or unreleased photos.

Keep the originals safe. Store unedited files separately from edited ones, so you can always go back and edit again for a different use or style. Name edited files clearly, for example by product and angle, so they are easy to find later.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have high-quality originals.
  • I have listed the edits for each image.
  • I know the sizes, formats and backgrounds I need.
  • I will approve a sample before the full batch.
  • Product colors and shapes will stay accurate.
