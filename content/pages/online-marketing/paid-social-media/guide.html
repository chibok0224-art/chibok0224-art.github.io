What a good paid social package includes

Paid social gigs cover ads on Facebook and Instagram, and often TikTok, Pinterest or LinkedIn. Most are sold as a campaign setup or as monthly management. The package should state:

  • Platforms covered, and which one comes first.
  • Campaign setup: number of campaigns and audiences, and the campaign goal (sales, leads, messages).
  • Tracking: pixel or conversion setup, and how it will be tested.
  • Creative: whether ad images, videos and text are included, or must come from you.
  • Testing plan: how different ads and audiences will be compared.
  • Reporting: what you will receive and how often.
  • Access: work done inside your own business and ad accounts.

How to brief a paid social expert

  1. The goal: online sales, leads, app installs, or messages to your business.
  2. Who buys from you: age range, location, interests and the problem you solve for them.
  3. Your offer: the product, price range and any current promotion.
  4. Your budget for ads and the limit you do not want to pass.
  5. Creative you already have: photos, videos, reviews and past ads, with notes on what worked.
  6. Access to your business account, ad account and website for tracking.
  7. Rules to follow: claims you cannot make, and anything your industry restricts.

Give new campaigns time before judging them. Ad platforms need some results to learn who responds, and early numbers often swing a lot. Agree with the specialist on when you will review the first results together, and which number you will use to decide what happens next.

What drives the price

  • one-time setup versus ongoing management
  • number of platforms, campaigns and audiences
  • whether ad creative is produced by the specialist
  • tracking setup from scratch, including for online stores
  • how much ad spend is managed
  • depth and frequency of reporting and testing

Red flags

  • Guaranteed sales, a fixed cost per result, or a promise of "going viral".
  • Requests for your personal login instead of access through your business account.
  • No plan for tracking, so results cannot be measured.
  • Reports that focus on likes and reach while ignoring sales or leads.
  • Ads that make claims your product cannot support. Ad platforms may reject them or restrict your account.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My business and ad accounts are in my name.
  • I have one clear goal for the first campaign.
  • I know whether the ad creative is included or must come from me.
  • Tracking setup and testing are part of the job.
  • I have set an ad budget limit, separate from the fee.
