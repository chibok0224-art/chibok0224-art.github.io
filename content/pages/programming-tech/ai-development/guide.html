What a good AI development project includes

AI development gigs cover anything from a small script that summarizes documents to a full AI feature inside your product. The project should state:

  • The task: exactly what the AI does, with inputs and expected outputs.
  • Approach: which AI models or services are used, and why.
  • Data: what data the tool uses, where it comes from and where it is stored.
  • Interface: a web app, an integration with your existing tools, or an API.
  • Test cases and how results will be checked.
  • Running costs: an estimate of model and hosting costs, paid by you.
  • Milestones you can test along the way.
  • Handover: source code, prompts, settings, documentation and account access.

How to brief an AI developer

  1. The problem: the task that takes too long or costs too much today.
  2. Examples: ten or more real inputs with the output you would want.
  3. What "good enough" means: how often it must be right, and what happens when it is not.
  4. Where it will be used: which people, tools and systems.
  5. Data limits: what the tool may and may not see or store.
  6. Budget for the build and for monthly running costs.
  7. Deadline, and any date you must show a working version.

Ask for a small proof of concept first: the core task working on your real examples, even without a polished interface. It shows quickly whether the idea works before you pay for the full build.

Keep a person in the loop wherever mistakes would be costly. An AI tool that drafts a reply, flags a document or suggests a category for someone to approve is often safer, and easier to trust, than one that acts alone. You can reduce the checks later once the results prove reliable.

What drives the price

  • how complex the task is and how accurate it must be
  • connections to your data, documents and existing systems
  • custom training or fine-tuning versus using existing models
  • a user interface versus a behind-the-scenes automation
  • security, privacy and hosting requirements
  • testing, monitoring and support after launch
  • ongoing model usage, paid separately to the provider

Red flags

  • Promises that the AI will be accurate every time.
  • No test cases or way to measure results.
  • Accounts and API keys held in the developer's name.
  • No estimate of running costs.
  • Plans to send your sensitive data to services without explaining where it goes.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have described the task with real examples and expected results.
  • We have agreed on test cases and what counts as good enough.
  • All service accounts and keys will be in my name.
  • I have an estimate of the monthly running costs.
  • I will receive the source code, prompts and documentation.
