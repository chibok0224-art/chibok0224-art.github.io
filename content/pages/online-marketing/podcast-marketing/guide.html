What a good podcast marketing package includes

Podcast marketing gigs range from a launch plan to monthly promotion. Check that the package states:

  • Listing work: title, description, artwork and categories.
  • Guest outreach: how many shows pitched.
  • Clips and social content.
  • Newsletter or website support.
  • Ads or cross-promotions, if included.
  • Reporting.
  • Duration.

Ask for a list of the types of shows the expert will pitch you to, with reasons. The best guest spots are on shows whose listeners match your audience, not simply the biggest shows available.

How to brief a podcast marketer

  1. Your show: topic, format and audience.
  2. Current numbers and your best episodes.
  3. Goals: listeners, sponsors, leads for your business.
  4. Assets: artwork, episodes, transcripts.
  5. Your availability for guest appearances.
  6. Budget.

Make your best episodes easy to find. New listeners often start with an episode recommended in a show description, a pinned post or your website. Point them to the episodes that show your podcast at its best.

Publish consistently. A regular schedule helps listeners build a habit and gives marketing something steady to promote.

Ask for show notes and episode titles that are written for search. Many people find podcasts by searching for a topic or a guest's name. Clear titles and short, descriptive notes help new listeners find the right episode in podcast apps and search engines, long after it is published.

What drives the price

  • number of pitches and guest spots
  • clip and social content production
  • ads and cross-promotions
  • website and newsletter work
  • campaign length
  • the expert's network in your topic area

Ads for podcasts can be run in podcast apps or on social media. Keep ad spend separate and measure whether it brings listeners who stay.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed download numbers.
  • Offers of reviews or ratings for money.
  • No explanation of how listeners will be reached.
  • Guest pitches to unrelated shows.
  • Reports without data from your hosting platform.

Sponsors increasingly check download quality. Inflated numbers from fake downloads can cost you sponsorships and your reputation.

Work with your guests. When a guest appears on your show, give them a short clip, a link and a ready-made post they can share with their own audience. Guests who share episodes bring listeners who already trust them, which is one of the most effective and affordable ways to grow. Ask the marketer to prepare these materials for every episode.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My show listing and best episodes are ready.
  • The plan targets shows and platforms my audience uses.
  • No fake downloads or reviews will be used.
  • I have time for guest appearances.
  • Results will come from my hosting platform data.
