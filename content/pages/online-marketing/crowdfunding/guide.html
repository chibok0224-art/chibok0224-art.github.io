What a good crowdfunding package includes

Crowdfunding gigs range from page writing to full campaign management. Check that the package states:

  • Scope: pre-launch, launch, or both.
  • Campaign page: writing, design and images.
  • Rewards and pricing advice.
  • Audience building: landing page and email list.
  • Ads and outreach, and who pays ad spend.
  • Fees: fixed, percentage, or both.
  • Reporting during the campaign.

Read the fee terms carefully. A percentage of funds raised may sound low-risk, but combined with ad spend and platform fees it can take a large share. Calculate what you keep in a realistic scenario.

How to brief a crowdfunding expert

  1. The product: what it is, stage of development, and a prototype.
  2. Costs: production, packaging, shipping and fees.
  3. Timeline: when backers will receive rewards.
  4. Existing audience: email list, social followers, community.
  5. Assets: photos, video, renders.
  6. Funding goal and how it was calculated.

Set a funding goal that covers real costs but is reachable. A campaign that reaches its goal early often attracts more backers; one that struggles can stall.

Be honest about risks and timelines on the campaign page. Backers are more forgiving of delays they were warned about than of surprises.

Ask the expert to review your prototype and campaign materials honestly before launch. Backers look closely at the video, images and rewards. If something looks unclear or too good to be true, it is better to hear it from your expert than to see it in comments during the campaign.

What drives the price

  • pre-launch length and audience building
  • page writing, design and video
  • ad management and ad spend
  • outreach to press and creators
  • percentage or fixed fee structure
  • the expert's track record in your category

Plan the budget for after the campaign too: manufacturing, shipping and backer support take time and money.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed funding.
  • Offers of fake backers or pledges.
  • Vague answers about fees.
  • No questions about shipping costs.
  • No pre-launch plan.

Rules differ by platform and country, for example on what can be offered as rewards and how taxes apply to funds raised. Check the platform's rules and ask a tax adviser about your situation.

Plan communication with backers after the campaign ends. Regular, honest updates about manufacturing and shipping keep backers patient and protect your reputation, especially if there are delays. Ask whether the expert offers support for updates, surveys and fulfillment planning, or recommends someone who does.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have a pre-launch plan and email list.
  • Reward prices include production, fees and shipping.
  • I understand every fee and percentage.
  • My timeline and risks are honest.
  • No fake backers or guarantees.
