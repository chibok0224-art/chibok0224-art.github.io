What a good display advertising package includes

Check that the offer clearly states:

  • Platforms and networks.
  • Targeting and audiences.
  • Placement controls and brand safety.
  • Creatives: sizes and versions.
  • Tracking setup.
  • Reporting and optimization.
  • Management fee and ad budget.

Ask for a placement report after the first weeks showing where ads ran. Excluding poor sites early saves budget and protects your brand.

Set frequency limits so people do not see your ads too often. Overexposure wastes money and can create negative feelings toward your brand.

Ask the expert for examples of campaigns with similar goals and budgets. Look at how they measured success and what they changed over time. Strong experts can explain why some placements or audiences worked and others did not.

How to brief a display advertising expert

  1. Goal: awareness, traffic, sales or retargeting.
  2. Audience and locations.
  3. Budget and test period.
  4. Brand assets and messages.
  5. Website and tracking access.
  6. Sites to avoid.
  7. Timeline.

Separate retargeting from prospecting. Retargeting past visitors usually performs very differently from reaching new audiences, and mixing them hides what works.

Prepare several creative versions. Testing different images, headlines and offers shows quickly which messages attract attention.

Make sure landing pages match the ads. Clicking a banner and landing on an unrelated page wastes the click and lowers results.

Keep ad accounts in your name with the expert added as a user. Your data and history stay with your business.

Consider privacy rules and consent when using retargeting. Many regions require clear cookie consent before tracking visitors for advertising. Make sure your website's consent setup works before campaigns start, so audiences are built legally and ads run smoothly.

What drives the price

  • platforms and networks
  • budget size
  • number of creatives
  • audience research
  • tracking setup
  • reporting frequency

A simple retargeting campaign costs much less to manage than programmatic campaigns across many audiences, formats and markets.

Ad spend is separate from the expert's fee; ask how the fee is calculated.

Red flags

  • No placement controls.
  • Accounts owned by the expert.
  • No frequency limits.
  • Reports with impressions only.
  • Promises of guaranteed sales.

Review results against your goal, not just clicks. Cheap clicks from poor placements can look good while bringing no customers.

Tips for a smoother project

Refresh creatives regularly, since performance often drops as people see the same ads.

Plan a learning period. Display campaigns often need a few weeks to gather enough data before results stabilize. Agree on what you will review after the test period and what results would justify a larger budget.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • My goal and test budget are clear.
  • Placement controls are in place.
  • Frequency limits are set.
  • Accounts are in my name.
  • Tracking measures real results.
