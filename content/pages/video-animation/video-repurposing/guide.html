What a good repurposing package includes

Repurposing gigs are usually priced by the number of clips and source length. Check that the package states:

  • Number of clips and their length.
  • Selection: who chooses the moments.
  • Format: vertical, square or horizontal.
  • Captions: accuracy and style.
  • Hooks: a strong opening line or on-screen text.
  • Branding: logo, colors and end card.
  • Turnaround and revisions.

Ask each clip to make sense on its own. Viewers who never saw the full video should understand the point within seconds; a clip that starts mid-sentence or depends on earlier context rarely performs well.

Ask for a simple naming system for delivered clips, such as the source video title, clip number and topic. When you have dozens of clips across several videos, clear names make scheduling and reporting much easier, and help you find a clip again when it becomes relevant.

How to brief a repurposing editor

  1. The source video and a transcript if you have one.
  2. Platforms for the clips.
  3. Topics or moments you know are strong, with time stamps.
  4. Brand elements: fonts, colors, logo.
  5. Examples of clips you like.
  6. Posting schedule.

Give feedback on the first batch before ordering more. Once the style, length and caption look are right, later batches can follow the same rules quickly and consistently.

Link clips back to the source. A short line in the caption or a pinned comment pointing to the full video helps turn clip viewers into subscribers or listeners.

Keep a list of which clips performed well. It shows what your audience responds to, which helps you choose topics for future long videos too.

What drives the price

  • number of clips and source length
  • caption style and accuracy
  • motion graphics and branding
  • multiple platform versions
  • clip selection included
  • turnaround time

Monthly packages often cost less per clip and let the editor learn your style. Start with one video to test before committing.

Red flags

  • Clips that start mid-sentence or lack context.
  • Captions with obvious errors.
  • The same template for every clip regardless of content.
  • Music with no license.
  • No explanation of how moments were chosen.

Check that clips do not change the meaning of what was said. Short edits can accidentally take statements out of context, which can damage trust with your audience or guests.

Tips for a smoother project

Mix clip types over time: a strong opinion, a practical tip, a funny moment and a surprising fact. Posting the same type of clip every day becomes repetitive for followers. A varied mix keeps your feed interesting and shows different sides of your content and the people in it.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have shared the source video and strong moments.
  • Each clip will make sense on its own.
  • Captions will be accurate and styled.
  • Clips will link back to the full video.
