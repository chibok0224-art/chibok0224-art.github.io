What a good custom song package includes

Custom song gigs are priced by length, production and vocals. Check that the package states:

  • Song length and structure.
  • Genre and style.
  • Lyrics written from your details.
  • Vocals: singer type, and whether AI is used.
  • Production quality: simple guitar or full band.
  • Revisions for lyrics and recording.
  • Usage rights.

Ask to approve the lyrics before recording. Changing words after the song is recorded usually means recording again, while lyric changes on paper are quick.

Decide whether you want a simple, heartfelt recording or a fully produced song. A single voice with guitar or piano can feel intimate and personal, while full production suits parties and videos. Choosing the style upfront helps the creator set the right expectations for price and delivery time, and avoids paying for production the occasion does not need.

How to brief a custom song creator

  1. The occasion and the date.
  2. Names, with a pronunciation voice note.
  3. Stories and details to include.
  4. Mood: funny, romantic, emotional, celebratory.
  5. Genre and reference songs.
  6. Things to avoid: topics or words.

Share a few specific memories rather than a long general description. A single line about a first meeting place or a shared joke makes a song feel personal in a way general praise never can.

Plan how the song will be played: at a wedding, at a party or sent privately. It affects length and style, and whether a lyric video or printed lyrics would make the gift more memorable.

Order early for important dates. Rush fees are common, and a relaxed timeline leaves room for a revision if something is not quite right.

What drives the price

  • song length
  • production style
  • professional vocals versus AI
  • number of revisions
  • extras like lyric videos
  • rush delivery

Business or public use of the song usually needs a broader license than a personal gift. Mention it before ordering.

Red flags

  • AI voices sold as human singers without disclosure.
  • Generic lyrics with your names inserted.
  • No lyric approval before recording.
  • Unclear usage rights.
  • Samples that all sound the same.

Listen to several full samples from the creator, not only short clips. Check vocal quality, lyrics and whether songs sound personal or like a template.

Keep the final files safe and consider asking for an instrumental version too. It can be useful for a live performance at the event, a slideshow or a video. Many creators offer these extras at a small cost when ordered with the original song rather than later.

Quick pre-order checklist

  • I have shared names, pronunciations and stories.
  • I will approve lyrics before recording.
  • I know whether vocals are human or AI.
  • Usage rights fit my plans.
  • I have ordered early enough for my date.
